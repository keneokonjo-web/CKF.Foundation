Where we work

Ogwashi-Ukwu and beyond

CKF assists less privileged Nigerians, particularly in Ogwashi-Ukwu, Delta State. Our work starts at home and reaches outward to people across Nigeria who are held back by poverty.

  • 01Access to education through scholarships and other support for indigent students, as well as technology training and other practical skills.
  • 02Access to primary health care.
  • 03Community welfare, such as environmental cleanups.
  • 04Support to SMEs and other job-creating activities for youth.
